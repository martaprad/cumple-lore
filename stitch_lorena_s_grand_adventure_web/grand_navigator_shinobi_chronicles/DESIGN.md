## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immersive, celebratory journey commemorating a monumental milestone: reaching Level 40. It merges the romantic exploration of high-seas piracy—inspired by uncharted Grand Line routes, sea charts, and maritime logbooks—with the discipline, mystique, and dramatic intensity of classic shinobi lore.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Audacious Discovery:** Every interaction feels like unfolding an ancient map, breaking a dormant seal, or discovering an uncharted island.
- **Heroic Provenance:** The experience treats the passage of time as veteran experience gained across treacherous seas and intense trials.
- **Ceremonial Reverence:** Rich textures, wax insignias, and golden filigrees convey pride, accomplishment, and mythic weight.

### Design Movement: Tactile Maritime-Shinobi Skeuomorphism
The interface blends physical skeuomorphic tangibility with modern spatial composition. Instead of flat surfaces or ungrounded neon effects, interfaces feel crafted from authentic artifacts: weathered vellum parchments, deep-sea abyssal depths, polished mahogany framing, beaten bronze accents, and vermilion sealing wax. 

Visual accents incorporate nautical compass roses (*Rosas de los Vientos*), aged rhumb lines, and stylized three-comma *tomoe* motifs interwoven into navigation dials, counters, and status badges.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in nautical nocturnal darkness, warmed by aged tactile materials, and punctuated by dramatic ceremonial pigments:

- **Abyssal Depths (Neutral Foundation):** Deep Navy (`#0A192F`) and Midnight Trench (`#06101E`) serve as the infinite maritime expanse behind the UI. Container tiers, sheets, and modal backdrop scrims emerge from these abyssal shades.
- **Aged Vellum & Cartography (Surface & Light Neutral):** Ancient Parchment (`#F4ECD8`), Sunken Vellum (`#EADBBE`), and Sea-worn Scroll (`#DFCEAA`) provide high-contrast legibility for log entries, question cards, and maps.
- **Worn Gold & Marine Bronze (Primary Accent):** Antiqued Gold (`#D4AF37`), Deep Bronze (`#C59B27`), and Shimmering Foil (`#F3E5AB`) represent achievements, active highlights, key borders, and royal navigational tools.
- **Cinnabar & Shinobi Lacquer (Secondary Accent):** Scarlet Seal (`#A31D1D`) and Deep Cinnabar (`#841616`) embody ninja seal imprints, wax tokens, critical alerts, and boss-level challenges.
- **Hidden All Blue & Jade Relic (Tertiary / Success):** Tropical Emerald (`#0D9488`), Island Lagoon (`#14B8A6`), and Deep Abyss Green (`#065F46`) are reserved for level reveals, correct answers, XP surges, and triumphant unlocks.
- **Vessel Rigging & Timber (Structural Low-Tone):** Rich Mahogany (`#3D2314`) and Smoked Timber (`#2A170A`) define card trims, progress bars, and header moldings.

## Typography

Typography balances narrative drama with ergonomic legibility during quest progression and quiz examination:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes nautical captain logs, royal decrees, and classical manga chapter title cards. High contrast serifs deliver gravity and prestige to level indicators, chapter markers, and victory declarations.
- **Body & Longform Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean readability on top of textured vellum or deep navy containers, preventing eye fatigue across long trivia prompts and lore dialogs.
- **Labels, Trackers & Data Gauges (`Outfit`):** Geometric, modern proportions provide crisp alignment for numeric statistics, coordinates, cooldowns, and nautical compass measurements.
- **Formatting Directives:** Headline treatments regularly feature uppercase track-spaced styling (`label-caps`) when denoting mission tiers, trial status, or ranking insignia.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances centered expedition stages with modular HUD panels reminiscent of an admiral's chart table.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column fluid grid system bounded at 1280px max-width, with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 40px (`2.5rem`) margins. Central examination cards take 8 columns, flanked by navigation gauges and shinobi seal trackers in 2-column sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** An 8-column layout with 24px gutters. Secondary dials collapse into sticky top navigation ribbon.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single-column flow with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and side margins. Cards span edge-to-edge with inset content padding to maximize reading area.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section transitions use `space-xl` (40px) to establish deliberate pauses between trials. Within components, question prompts and interactive options align to regular `space-md` (16px) rhythms.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and physical rather than abstract, simulating layered physical sea charts, wooden chart desks, and raised metallic relics:

- **Surface 0 (The Sea / Background):** Radial wash transitioning from Midnight Trench (`#06101E`) to Deep Navy (`#0A192F`), overlaid with subtle 4% opacity gold navigational latitude lines.
- **Surface 1 (Chart Table & Wood Trim):** Deep Mahogany (`#3D2314`) with a sharp 1px top highlight (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`) and deep drop shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(2, 6, 12, 0.8)`.
- **Surface 2 (Vellum Scroll / Active Card):** Layered Pergamino parchment (`#F4ECD8` to `#EADBBE`) casting a dual ambient shadow: an inner edge bevel (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6), inset 0 -2px 4px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.25)`) and a diffuse grounding drop shadow `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Surface 3 (Tokens, Relics & Floating Medallions):** Elevated interactive widgets featuring warm bronze drop shadows (`0 8px 16px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`) and micro-beveled metallic rim borders.

## Shapes

The interface employs a disciplined **Soft (`1`)** roundedness profile to evoke parchment sheets, chiselled wooden planks, and clipped wax seals:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to standard badges, data inputs, tags, and small inventory slots, mirroring cut paper or stamped leather corners.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to question cards, logbook panels, and modal containers.
- **Pill & Circular Exceptions:** Reserved strictly for circular seal stamps (Hanko / Tomoe badges), wax seal triggers, compass roses, and level avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Seals
- **Primary ("Level Up / Seal Break"):** Warm gradient from Beaten Gold (`#D4AF37`) to Dark Bronze (`#C59B27`) with a 1px outer rim of Shimmering Foil (`#F3E5AB`). Text is uppercase `Outfit` bold in Midnight Navy (`#06101E`). Pressed state simulates a physical coin plunge with inner shadow `inset 0 3px 6px rgba(42, 23, 10, 0.6)`.
- **Secondary ("Shinobi Jutsu / Clue"):** Deep Scarlet Lacquer (`#A31D1D`) with etched vermilion borders and antique parchment typography.
- **Ghost Action:** Outlined in 1px weathered gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) with transparent backdrop, gaining a subtle amber wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`) on hover.

### Examination & Question Cards
- Bound in dual borders: an outer 2px Mahogany frame (`#3D2314`) and an inner 1px hairline border of Gold Foil (`#D4AF37`) offset by 4px.
- Background uses aged parchment tones (`#F4ECD8`) with charcoal-brown ink typography (`#2A170A`) for optimal readability.
- Corner filigrees subtly display nautical knotwork transitioning into stylized ninja shuriken flourishes.

### Interactive Choice Selectors (Radios / Checkboxes)
- **Default State:** Weathered parchment container with 1px bronze border. Indicator is an empty bronze ring.
- **Selected State:** Border illuminates in Emerald Lagoon (`#0D9488`) or Golden Amber (`#D4AF37`). The radio fill is stamped with a solid Cinnabar wax seal containing a single *tomoe* comma symbol.

### Progress & Log Pose Navigation Bars
- Modeled after a bronze log pose tube. Track background is deep mahogany with an engraved groove.
- Fill is a dual-tone glowing gradient from Tropical Emerald (`#0D9488`) to Island Turquoise (`#14B8A6`), capped with a miniature golden galleon or shuriken marker.

### Input Fields
- Styled like empty logbook lines or parchment ledger boxes: Background `#EADBBE`, inner inset shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(42, 23, 10, 0.15)`, and bottom-weighted bronze border. Focus creates an ethereal cyan-emerald chakra glow.

### Level 40 Reveal Artifact (Specialty Component)
- A full-screen or modular scroll artifact combining five wax seals that break dynamically. Features spinning concentric brass rings inspired by armillary spheres and ninja summoning circles, opening into a celebratory emerald-and-gold treasure chamber showcase.
